# 📊 Financial Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Financial Sales Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, financial metrics, employee productivity, product performance, and marketing effectiveness. It enables businesses to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify trends, and make informed business decisions through dynamic visualizations and interactive slicers.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance and profitability.
- Monitor key financial KPIs.
- Identify top-performing products and employees.
- Evaluate marketing channel effectiveness.
- Compare revenue across different product categories.
- Provide actionable business insights and recommendations.

## 🔍 Business Insights

- Mocha generated the highest revenue among all products.
- Kiran achieved the highest revenue among employees.
- Facebook was the most effective marketing channel based on revenue.
- Coffee contributed the highest revenue among all product categories.
- Revenue is concentrated among a few high-performing products, indicating strong demand for selected items.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Increase promotional campaigns for top-performing products such as **Mocha**.
- Invest more marketing budget in high-performing channels like **Facebook**.
- Improve the performance of low-selling products through discounts, bundle offers, or seasonal campaigns.
- Analyze customer preferences to optimize product offerings.
- Monitor employee performance regularly to improve sales productivity.
